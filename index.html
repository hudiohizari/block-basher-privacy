Block Basher Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Block Basher is a block puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information the game keeps on your device

Block Basher has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos. Your high score, settings (sound and screen shake), tutorial progress and an in-progress game are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version,
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can accept, refuse, or choose individual purposes, and change your choice later under Settings → Privacy Options.
  • US states with privacy laws (such as California, Colorado, Virginia and Texas): you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising through the "My data preferences" choice the game shows.
  • Everywhere else (including Brazil, Canada, Indonesia and India): you can turn off ad personalisation by deleting your advertising ID as described below, and you may contact us to exercise any rights your local law gives you.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Without it, ads are not personalised.
  • You can stop all data use by the game at any time by uninstalling it.

Children

Block Basher is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

What we do not do

We do not run our own servers for the game, do not collect analytics of our own, and do not sell your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
